Under the hood

Three models, one recommendation

👥

Co-reading

Item-kNN finds books that the same readers read together: "readers of X also read Y".

🏷️

Tags & authors

A content model compares Goodreads tags and authors with your taste profile.

⭐

Star predictions

SVD matrix factorisation predicts how many stars you would give each book.
